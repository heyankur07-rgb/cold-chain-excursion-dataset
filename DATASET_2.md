# Dataset Description

## Overview

A synthetic but physically motivated log of **64,052 pharmaceutical cold-chain shipments** dispatched between January 2024 and June 2026 from 10 manufacturing and distribution sites to 21 destination countries.

Each row is one insulated shipper (box), equipped with a temperature data logger. Shippers that are packed together and move as one unit share a `consignment_id`. The label `excursion` is 1 when the shipper's payload left its required temperature range during transit, too warm or too cold. In practice, an excursion means the goods are quarantined and usually destroyed.

The data was produced by `generate_data.py`, which is fully reproducible given its seed, with no external inputs. The seed is withheld so the data cannot be regenerated by benchmark participants; reviewers can request it. The generator models:

- **Transit time against packaging holdover.** Every passive box keeps the payload in range only for a nominal number of hours.
- **Ambient heat and cold.** These depend on month, hemisphere and the climate at the origin, destination and each air hub. Tarmac time at hot hubs adds stress.
- **Pre-conditioning quality.** This is the temperature recorded at pack-out.
- **Packing events.** These are recorded as free-text handler notes.
- **Carrier quality.** One carrier is noticeably worse.
- **Active-container power faults.**
- **A shared consignment-level shock.** Boxes in the same consignment have correlated outcomes.

No LLM output was used anywhere. All text comes from fixed templates inside the generator. No third-party data is used. The dataset and its generator are released under CC0 1.0.

**Overall excursion rate:** about 13%. The rate varies strongly by packaging, roughly 2% for active containers versus about 55% for 24-hour EPS gel boxes.

## File Structure

- `data.csv` (14.1 MB): the full labelled shipment log. 64,052 rows (shippers) in 26,000 consignments, 28 columns, one row per shipper.
- `generate_data.py`: the reproducible generator. `python generate_data.py --seed <SEED> --out data.csv` recreates `data.csv` byte for byte with the withheld seed.

## Features

| Column | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| shipment_id | str | Unique shipper identifier (`SHP` + 8 hex characters) |
| consignment_id | str | Group of 1–6 shippers packed and moved together. They share dispatch time, route, carrier, product and packaging. |
| dispatch_ts | str | Dispatch timestamp. The format depends on the origin site: `dd/mm/YYYY HH:MM` for BSL, GNT, ORK, LYS, PNQ, HYD and GRU, and `YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM` for RDU, IND and SIN. |
| origin_site | str | Origin site code (IATA-like, 10 values) |
| destination_country | str | ISO-2 destination country (21 values). If it equals the origin's country, the shipment is domestic. |
| route_hubs | str | Pipe-delimited list of transit air hubs in order, e.g. `FRA\|DXB`. Empty means a direct or road shipment. |
| carrier | str | Logistics carrier (6 values) |
| service_level | str | PRIORITY, STANDARD or ECONOMY |
| product_class | str | VACCINE, INSULIN, MAB_BIOLOGIC, CELL_THERAPY, MRNA_BULK or DIAGNOSTIC_KIT |
| temp_range | str | Required range: `2-8C`, `-20C` (frozen) or `15-25C` (controlled room temperature) |
| packaging_code | str | Shipper type. The trailing `NNH` is the nominal holdover in hours, e.g. `PCM28-96H` means 96 h. `ACTIVE-RKN` is a powered, actively cooled container with no fixed holdover. `PCM28-72H` was introduced in June 2025. |
| payload_units | int | Number of product units in the box |
| gross_weight_kg | float | Gross weight of the shipper |
| logger_model | str | Temperature logger model: TL-20, SenseQ-4 or TempTrak-US |
| precool_reading | float | Payload temperature recorded at pack-out. **TempTrak-US loggers report °F. The other models report °C.** |
| planned_transit_h | float | Planned door-to-door transit time in hours, excluding customs |
| expected_customs_h | float | Expected customs dwell in hours, estimated at booking. **Blank for domestic shipments** (no customs), not unknown. |
| forecast_max_temp_origin_c | float | Forecast daily maximum temperature (°C) at the origin on the dispatch date |
| forecast_max_temp_dest_c | float | Forecast daily maximum temperature (°C) at the destination on the dispatch date |
| handler_notes | str | Free-text notes written by the packing team at dispatch, with mixed casing and phrasing. May be blank. |
| label_printer_id | str | ID of the printer that printed the shipping label. Carries no signal. |
| record_checksum | str | Row checksum. Unique per row, carries no signal. |
| actual_transit_h | float | **Recorded after arrival.** Actual transit time, including customs. |
| logger_peak_status | str | **Recorded after arrival.** Summary of the logger trace against the required range: IN_RANGE, ABOVE_RANGE or BELOW_RANGE. |
| logger_alarm_level | str | **Recorded after arrival.** Logger alarms triggered in transit: NONE, SINGLE or MULTIPLE. |
| qa_disposition | str | **Recorded after arrival.** RELEASED or QUARANTINED. |
| insurance_claim_filed | str | **Recorded after arrival.** YES if an insurance claim was filed, else NO. |
| excursion | int | **Target.** 1 if a temperature excursion occurred, else 0. |

## Data Characteristics

- **Target distribution:** imbalanced, with 8,300 excursions out of 64,052 rows (13.0%). The rate is 13.5% for shipments dispatched in 2024–2025 and 11.2% for January–June 2026 (winter and spring months). By range: 2–8 °C 12.2%, 15–25 °C 22.7%, −20 °C 4.1%.
- **Missing values:**
  - `route_hubs` is blank in 13.2% of rows (no transit hub).
  - `handler_notes` is blank in 4.0%.
  - `expected_customs_h` is blank in 2.1%, exactly the domestic shipments.
- **No-signal columns:** `record_checksum` is unique per row. `label_printer_id` (39 values) is random.
- **Mixed units:** 29.7% of `precool_reading` values come from TempTrak-US loggers and are in °F.
- **Mixed date formats:** `dispatch_ts` is `dd/mm/YYYY HH:MM` or `YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM` depending on `origin_site`. A generic parser silently reads the day as the month for dates on days 1–12.
- **Pipe-separated values:** `route_hubs` holds 0–3 hubs from 15 possible hub codes.
- **Text feature:** `handler_notes` holds templated free text in mixed case. It may join several notes with `;`.
- **Encoded numeric value:** `packaging_code` ends with the holdover in hours (`NNH`).
- **Grouping:** consignments hold 1–6 shippers (mean 2.5), and their outcomes are correlated.

## Notes

- **Prediction time is dispatch.** Only information known when the box leaves the dock can be used to predict risk. The five post-arrival columns are kept in the raw log because real shipment histories contain them, but they are direct consequences of the label. `logger_alarm_level` and `logger_peak_status` each separate the classes almost perfectly.
- **Excursions go both ways.** For 2–8 °C goods in passive boxes, winter cold can freeze the payload, and that is also an excursion. Heat is the dominant driver overall.
- **Hemisphere matters.** GRU (Brazil), KE, ZA and AU are in the southern hemisphere, so their seasons are reversed.
- **Drift.** Volumes grow over time, a new packaging type appears in mid-2025, and the seasonal mix differs between periods.
- **Duplicate-looking rows** within a consignment are legitimate, separate shippers.
